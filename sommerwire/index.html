Transmission lines

An ideal, lossless line between two feed points, connected across the gap at each end like NEC's TL card. It does not radiate or couple to the wires, so it suits coax. Leave Length empty for the straight distance between the ends; the velocity factor makes the line electrically longer. Reversed swaps the two conductors at the far end. A source on a line end drives the line and the wire gap together.

Buildings

A house beside the antenna. X and Y are the centre of the footprint, Length runs along the ridge, Rotation turns the ridge away from the X axis. A metal roof is solved as a wire grid (mesh about λ/10 at the highest frequency or finer: 1 m suits HF); finer meshes are slower. Walls are shown for reference only: stone, brick and wood are not conductors, and a wire solver cannot model them. Metal gutters run along both eaves (not bonded to the roof); downpipes run from the gutter ends into the ground, each with an earth resistance (typically tens to hundreds of ohms at HF; 0 = perfect). If your downpipes end in plastic drains, choose None. Wiring or a steel frame can be drawn as wires. Add height on the Calculate tab lifts buildings too.

Conditions

With Real ground, the impedance is solved with Sommerfeld integrals, as NEC-2 does: exact for a flat homogeneous ground, so wires a few centimetres above the soil (radials, low dipoles, feed lines) come out right, ground loss included. "Complex image" is a faster approximation that is good above about 0.1 λ but underestimates ground loss close to the soil. "Perfect mirror" reproduces MININEC and MMANA-GAL. If any wire touches the ground, the whole impedance is solved over the perfect mirror; buried wires are not supported. The far field uses Fresnel reflection. Tapered segments run from λ/(SC·DM1) at wire ends to λ/DM2 in the middle.

    Sommerwire by ON9PK Koen Van den Heuvel · free and unencumbered software, released into the public domain (Unlicense)

    Sommerwire is an independent thin-wire moment-method solver in the MININEC style (pulse currents at nodes, reduced kernel, image ground). It reads and writes the plain-text .maa layout used by MMANA-GAL so you can bring models across, but it is not MMANA-GAL and its numbers can differ slightly. The optimizer is a bounded Nelder–Mead search, restarted with smaller steps.
